What is Background Remover?

The Background Remover uses an AI segmentation model to automatically detect the subject of your image and remove everything else. Upload any photo — a portrait, product shot, logo, or object — and the tool isolates the main subject and produces a transparent PNG cutout. Unlike manual color-picking tools, this uses machine learning to understand the actual content of the image, handling complex edges like hair, fur, and semi-transparent objects. The entire process runs in your browser with no server uploads.

How to Use

  1. Upload your image — Drag and drop or click to browse. Supports PNG, JPG, WebP, BMP, and GIF.
  2. Wait for processing — The AI model analyzes your image and separates the subject from the background. The first use downloads the model (~30 MB), which is then cached.
  3. Review the result — See the original and result side by side. The checkerboard pattern shows where the background has been removed.
  4. Download — Click "Download Transparent PNG" to save your cutout image. The file preserves the original resolution.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. The AI model runs entirely in your browser using WebAssembly. Your images never leave your device. This is fundamentally different from cloud-based tools that send your images to remote servers for processing.

Why does the first use take longer?

On the first visit, the tool downloads the AI model weights (~30 MB). Your browser caches these files, so subsequent visits load much faster. The actual image processing typically takes 2-10 seconds depending on image size and device performance.

What types of images work best?

The tool works well with portraits, product photos, animals, objects, and any image where there is a clear subject. It handles complex edges like hair and fur well. Very busy images with no clear subject-background distinction may produce less accurate results.

Can I process multiple images?

Yes. After downloading your result, click "Remove Another" to process a new image. Since the model is already cached, subsequent images process quickly.

What resolution is the output?

The output preserves the original image resolution. A 4000x3000 photo will produce a 4000x3000 transparent PNG.

Does it work on mobile devices?

Yes, though processing may be slower on mobile devices due to limited computational resources. The tool works in all modern mobile browsers.
