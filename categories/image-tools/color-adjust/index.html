What is Color Adjust?

Color Adjust is a free browser-based image editing tool that lets you fine-tune the visual appearance of any image. Using intuitive sliders you can increase or decrease brightness, boost or reduce contrast, and shift saturation from fully desaturated to hyper-vivid. Toggle buttons let you instantly convert an image to grayscale or invert all its colors. A live CSS-filter preview updates in real time as you drag sliders, so you see the result immediately without waiting for pixel-level processing. When you are satisfied, the export engine applies the same adjustments at the pixel level for a high-quality downloadable PNG. All computation runs locally in your browser. Your images are never uploaded to any server.

How to Use

  1. Upload an Image: Click the upload area or drag and drop an image file. Supported formats include PNG, JPG, WebP, BMP, and GIF.
  2. Adjust Sliders: Drag the Brightness, Contrast, and Saturation sliders to modify the image. Changes are previewed instantly using hardware-accelerated CSS filters.
  3. Toggle Effects: Click the Grayscale or Invert buttons to enable or disable those effects. Active effects are highlighted in red.
  4. Compare: Switch to the Before/After tab to see the original and adjusted images side by side.
  5. Reset: Click Reset to restore all adjustments to their default values.
  6. Download: Click Download to export the adjusted image as a PNG file. The export applies pixel-level processing for accurate color reproduction.

Common Uses

Photo Correction

Fix underexposed or overexposed photos by adjusting brightness and contrast. Recover detail in shadows or highlights without specialized software.

Social Media Graphics

Quickly enhance product photos, thumbnails, or banner images before posting. Boost saturation for eye-catching visuals or desaturate for a muted aesthetic.

Game Asset Preparation

Adjust sprite and texture colors for different environments or factions. Convert to grayscale for disabled-state UI elements or invert for negative-space effects.

Accessibility Testing

Preview how images look in grayscale or inverted mode to evaluate accessibility and contrast for users with visual impairments.
