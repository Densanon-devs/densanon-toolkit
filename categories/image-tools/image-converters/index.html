Frequently Asked Questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Every conversion runs entirely in your browser using the HTML Canvas API. Your images never leave your device — there is no server upload, no cloud processing, and no data collection.

Which format gives the smallest file size?

For photographs, WebP typically produces the smallest files at equivalent quality, followed by JPG. For graphics with flat colors and sharp edges, PNG can sometimes be smaller than JPG. BMP is always the largest since it stores raw uncompressed pixel data.

Will I lose image quality when converting?

Converting to lossless formats (PNG, BMP) preserves exact pixel data. Converting to lossy formats (JPG, WebP) may reduce quality depending on the quality setting — higher settings preserve more detail. Converting from lossy to lossless does not restore lost quality; it only prevents further degradation.

What is the maximum file size I can convert?

There is no hard limit. Since processing happens in your browser, the practical limit depends on your device's available memory. Most images under 50MB convert without issues. Very large images (100MB+) may cause slower browsers to struggle.

Can I convert multiple images at once?

Each converter handles one image at a time to give you full control over quality settings and preview comparison. For batch conversion, convert each image individually — the process is instant so it's quick even for several files.

Why does my converted image have a white background?

If your original image has transparency (common in PNG and WebP) and you convert to JPG or BMP, the transparent areas are filled with white because these formats do not support transparency. Convert to PNG or WebP instead if you need to keep transparency.
