What Does This Tool Do?

This tool converts SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics) files to JPG (JPEG) format directly in your browser. SVG is a vector format used for logos, icons, and graphics that scale to any size. JPG is the most widely supported raster image format, optimized for photographs and web images with adjustable compression. This converter renders your SVG onto a canvas and exports it as a compressed JPG image, ideal for sharing on platforms that require raster images.

SVG vs JPG — Key Differences

SVG stores images as mathematical shapes and paths, making them infinitely scalable. JPG is a lossy raster format that stores images as a grid of pixels with compression. SVG files are best for graphics with clean lines and flat colors, while JPG excels at photographs and complex images. JPG does not support transparency — any transparent areas in your SVG will be filled with a white background during conversion.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will transparent areas in my SVG become white?

Yes. JPG does not support transparency, so any transparent regions in your SVG will be rendered with a white background.

How much smaller will the JPG be compared to the SVG?

File size depends on the SVG complexity and JPG quality setting. Simple SVGs may actually be smaller than their JPG equivalents, while complex SVGs with many gradients typically produce compact JPGs.

Is my file uploaded to a server?

No. The entire conversion happens locally in your browser. Your files never leave your device.
