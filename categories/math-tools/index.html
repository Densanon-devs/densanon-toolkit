Math Tools

Calculators, visualizers, and mathematical utilities for students and teachers.

Our free online math tools help you calculate, convert, and visualize numbers instantly in your browser — no signup, no downloads, no ads. Whether you need to convert a decimal to a fraction, calculate a percentage, simplify a ratio, or find the mean of a dataset, every tool runs entirely in your browser and delivers instant results. Students, teachers, and professionals use these tools daily for homework, lesson planning, data analysis, and everyday math.

Fraction Visualizer

Show fractions as circle, bar, or grid diagrams. Compare two fractions side by side, see decimals, percentages, and equivalent fractions.

Percent Calculator

Calculate percentages three ways: X% of Y, what percent X is of Y, and the percentage change between two values.

Decimal to Fraction Converter

Convert any decimal to a simplified fraction with step-by-step breakdown. Supports repeating decimals like 0.333...

Fraction to Decimal Converter

Convert fractions and mixed numbers to decimals. Detects terminating vs repeating patterns and shows the repeating cycle.

Ratio Simplifier

Simplify 2-part or 3-part ratios, scale to a target value, and generate equivalent ratios.

Mean / Median / Mode Calculator

Enter a list of numbers and get mean, median, mode, range, sum, and more with a visual bar chart.
