Privacy & your data

Your decks never leave your machine. Densa Deck is a local desktop app, not a web service. All analysis, simulation, and AI coaching runs on your own CPU/GPU.

  • No account, no login. Your license key is validated offline.
  • No telemetry. The app never phones home during normal use. The only network calls are: card-database fetch from Scryfall (once at setup), optional AI model download from Hugging Face (once if you opt in), and an optional check for updates against toolkit.densanon.com.
  • Decklists, analysis results, saved versions, and coach conversations live in %USERPROFILE%\.densa-deck\ on your machine. Delete the folder to wipe everything.
  • Stripe processes the payment. We receive the Stripe session ID (used to derive your license key deterministically) and your email — nothing else. Your card number never touches our servers because we don't have servers.

Frequently asked

What happens if I lose my license key?

Your Stripe receipt (the email Stripe sent after checkout) contains a link back to the receipt page. The license key is deterministically derived from your Stripe session ID, so if you can get to the success page URL from your browser history OR Stripe receipt, the same key re-appears. If neither of those works, email admin@densanon.com with your Stripe receipt and we'll resend it within 48 hours.

Can I use one license on multiple machines?

Yes. Your license key is the same on every machine — there's no per-device lockout and no seat limit. Install Densa Deck on your desktop, laptop, and anywhere else you'd like to use it. We trust you not to share the key publicly.

How do updates work?

The app checks for new versions when it launches and shows a banner if one's available. Click the banner to go to the download page. Extract the new ZIP to the same folder (replacing the old files) — your card database, saved decks, coach sessions, and license key stay intact (they live in %USERPROFILE%\.densa-deck\, outside the app folder).

What's your refund policy?

Full refund within 14 days, no questions asked. The 3 free browser tools on this site are meant to give you a feel for the ranking + analysis quality before buying — if the Pro app still doesn't click for you, email admin@densanon.com with your Stripe receipt and we'll refund via Stripe.

What formats does it support?

Primary focus: Commander / EDH (100-card singleton). Also works for Modern, Legacy, Vintage, Standard, Pioneer, and Pauper — the static analysis and goldfish engines are format-aware, and the staples checker has a Commander staples list built in. Format-specific features (bans, legality) respect each format's rules.

The Windows "SmartScreen" warning — is this safe?

Yes. The binary isn't yet signed with a paid code-signing certificate (those cost $120–$700/year; we're adding one once sales justify it). Windows treats any unsigned app as suspicious by default. Click More info → Run anyway to proceed. You can verify the file's SHA-256 hash against the value published on the GitHub release page if you want extra assurance.

How do I uninstall?

v0.1.0 is a portable ZIP — no Windows uninstaller. Just delete the densa-deck\ folder you extracted. To also wipe your card database, saved decks, and license key, delete %USERPROFILE%\.densa-deck\. That's everything Densa Deck ever wrote to your machine.

Is this an official Wizards of the Coast product?

No. Densa Deck is an independent tool built by Densanon LLC. It's not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Wizards of the Coast LLC. All card data is fetched from Scryfall, which publishes it under agreement with Wizards. No card images are hosted by us — we hotlink to Scryfall for any image display.

Email admin@densanon.com — include your Stripe receipt (or order ID) if it's a license issue, and your Windows version + a short description of what you tried if it's an install issue. Response within 48 hours.
