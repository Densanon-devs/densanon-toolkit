Power Level Rating

Goldfish Simulation

Run thousands of solo games to measure how consistently your deck executes. Kill turn distribution, mulligan rates, objective pass rates.

Matchup Gauntlet

Test against 11 archetype opponents (aggro, control, combo, stax, cEDH, and more). See your weighted meta positioning and weak matchups.

Version Tracking

Save deck snapshots, compare any two versions, see score deltas and trend analysis. Track your deck's improvement over time.

Castability Analysis

Per-card mana base check. Find demanding cards you can't reliably cast on curve and get specific fixes for your mana base.

Report Export

Export full analysis as JSON, Markdown, or styled HTML reports. Perfect for sharing builds, getting feedback, or archiving.

Interactive training mode. Draw hands, decide keep or mull, and the engine scores your decisions to sharpen your intuition.

Smart Recommendations
